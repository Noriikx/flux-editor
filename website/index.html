Questions

Is Flux safe to install?

Yes. Your videos stay on your computer. Flux only goes online for your sign-in, free music and the caption model. Passwords are stored encrypted. Payments are handled by Stripe, so Flux never sees your card.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. What do I do?

That message appears for new apps that aren't widely downloaded yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

My Mac says Flux can't be opened.

In Finder, right-click Flux in Applications and choose Open, then Open again. You only need to do this once.

What computer do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or macOS 12 or newer. 4 GB of memory works; 8 GB is smoother for HD video. Flux lowers the preview quality automatically on slower laptops - exports always stay full quality.

Do captions work offline?

Yes, after a one-time download of the speech model (40-250 MB depending on the accuracy you pick). Your audio never leaves your computer.

Is Premium a subscription?

Yes, €4.99 a month. Cancel anytime with the link in your Stripe receipt email - Premium stays on until the paid month ends.
