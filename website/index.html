Features

Everything you reach for, right where you expect it.

Drag clips in, cut with one key, and see every change instantly. The sound of each video sits under it on its own track, so you can move, trim and mix it separately.

Sound you can see

Waveforms on every clip. Drag the volume line, drag fade handles, and press Balance audio so a loud mic and a quiet one end up the same.

Cut and trim fast

Split with S, drag edges to trim, snap clips together, close every gap in one click.

Auto captions

Speech turns into subtitles in 28 styles. Runs on your PC - nothing is uploaded.

Templates

Vlog, fast TikTok, travel, gaming and more. Add your clips and press Fill.

Free music library

2,000+ free songs, with the licence shown and the credit text written for you.

80+ transitions

Crossfades, slides, wipes, hearts, glitch, spiral, TV switch and more. Click the + between two clips.

Keyframes & graph editor

Animate size, position, rotation and more, then shape the curves - free for everyone.

Masks & effects

Masks, 48 effects and 60+ looks - blur or pixelate just one part, glow, VHS, night vision and more.

Voiceovers & voices

Record over your video with a live level meter, or use 10 natural text-to-speech voices.

Audio effects

50+ effects: ultra loud, 8-bit, robot, cave, cathedral, chipmunk, EQ, reverb, noise reduction and more.

Fast & smooth

Instant scrubbing, smooth playback up to 10x speed, and a light preview mode for older laptops.

Viral Shorts PREMIUM

Drop in a long video or podcast. Flux finds the best 30-60 second moments and turns each one into a ready vertical Short with captions.

FAQ

Questions

Something else? Email us or use Settings → Report a bug in the app.

Is Flux safe to install?

Yes. Your videos stay on your computer and are never uploaded. Flux only goes online to sign you in, check for updates and download free extras you ask for (music, fonts, the caption model and the voices). Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, never in plain text. See the Privacy Policy.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. What do I do?

That message appears for new apps that aren't widely downloaded yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

My Mac says Flux can't be opened.

In Finder, right-click Flux in Applications and choose Open, then Open again. You only need to do this once.

What computer do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or macOS 12 or newer. 4 GB of memory works; 8 GB is smoother for HD video. Flux lowers the preview quality automatically on slower laptops - exports always stay full quality.

Do captions work offline?

Yes, after a one-time download of the speech model (40-250 MB depending on the accuracy you pick). Your audio never leaves your computer.

How do I get Premium?

You can't buy it yet - Premium is coming soon. No payments are taken right now. Everything in the free plan works without an account limit, trial or watermark.
