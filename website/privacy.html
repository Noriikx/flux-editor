Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026 (updated for Flux 1.7.1). This explains what data Flux uses, why, and your rights.

The short version:
  • Your videos, projects and recordings stay on your computer. We never upload them.
  • For your account we keep your email, a scrambled (hashed) password and a few account details.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no session recording, and we never sell your data.
  • The AI Editor, captions and voices run on your own computer.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is the independent developer of Flux, based in Romania. Contact: flux.editing.app@gmail.com. This policy covers the Flux desktop app, the website flux-editor.netlify.app and the Flux account service.

2. What stays on your computer

Everything you make in Flux is stored only on your own computer, including:

  • your projects, imported media, voice recordings and exports;
  • project version history (the Time machine), the Trash (deleted projects, kept for 30 days) and your settings;
  • your achievements and stats (how many videos you exported, time spent editing and so on). They are counted on your computer and never sent to us;
  • speech-to-text for captions, bleeping, silence removal and Viral Shorts. The model runs locally, so your audio isn't sent anywhere;
  • Studio sound, Improve quality, AI upscale and AI slow motion. They run on your own computer;
  • text to speech voices. They are made on your computer, so your text isn't sent anywhere;
  • the AI Editor, unless you add your own Anthropic key (see section 4).

4. AI Editor

The AI Editor works on your computer. Your requests and your project are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Optional: if you add your own Anthropic API key in Settings → AI, your typed request and a text summary of your project (such as clip names, lengths, positions, text and caption words) go directly from your computer to Anthropic, which runs the AI model. Your video and audio files are never sent. This happens only with your own key, under your own agreement with Anthropic and its privacy policy. We don't see or store any of it.

6. Things the app downloads

To give you free content, Flux downloads files from other websites. Like any web request, these sites can see your IP address and that a download happened, but we don't send them your email or your projects:

  • Music from incompetech.com and the Internet Archive (archive.org), where the free songs are stored;
  • Extra fonts from Google Fonts, only after you allow it (Flux asks the first time; change it any time in Settings → General);
  • The speech model for captions from Hugging Face (once, when you first make captions);
  • The text to speech voices from GitHub (once, when you turn them on);
  • The AI upscale and AI slow motion engines from GitHub (once each, when you first use them);
  • Links you paste into Viral Shorts (a video / audio file or a podcast feed), from the website you got the link from. Only when you paste one. For YouTube and Twitch links, the desktop app first downloads the free open-source tool yt-dlp from GitHub (checked with a secure hash), then the video from YouTube or Twitch.
  • Update checks and downloads from GitHub.

Downloads are checked with secure hashes where possible. The website doesn't load anything from other companies: its fonts are stored on our own site. The one exception is Flux Mobile (flux-editor.netlify.app/lite): when you make captions or Viral Shorts there, it downloads the speech engine from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) and the speech model from Hugging Face, once. Your video is never uploaded: the listening happens on your phone. If you sign in on Flux Mobile, your sign-in is kept in your browser's storage on that device until you sign out.

While you export, Flux runs a small local server that only your own computer can reach (127.0.0.1). It never accepts connections from the internet.

7. Cookies and local storage

The website uses no cookies and no tracking. That's why there's no cookie banner. The app stores your settings, projects and sign-in token in files on your computer. This is strictly needed for the app to work and is never used for tracking. See the Licenses & credits page for the open-source parts of Flux.

8. What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no tracking cookies on the website or in the app.
  • No session recording or screen replay tools. We don't record what you click or type.
  • We never sell or rent your data, and we don't share it except with the services listed above or when the law requires it.
  • We take no payments, so we have no payment or card data.

9. How long we keep data

  • Account data, profile picture and your friends/followers lists: until you delete your account. Then they're removed from our database straight away. Your username disappears from other people's lists too.
  • Shared templates: until you ask us to remove them or we reject them (rejected templates are deleted within 90 days).
  • One-time codes: until used or after 15 minutes.
  • Server logs at our hosting provider: kept for a short time under Netlify's own rules.
  • Email copies: emails we send may stay in the sending Gmail account's "Sent" folder. We delete them on request.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • correct it;
  • have it deleted (you can do this yourself in Flux: Account → Delete account);
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • take it with you (data portability).

Email flux.editing.app@gmail.com for any of these and we'll answer within one month. You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro), or to the authority in your own country.

11. Children

You must be at least 13 to have a Flux account. People aged 13 to 15 need a parent or guardian to agree, and confirm this when signing up. We only store the year you were born. If you think a child under 13 (or under 16 without permission) created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

12. Security

All connections to our server use HTTPS, and passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Sign-in codes are also stored hashed and are rate-limited. No system is 100% secure, so use a password you don't use anywhere else.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your data, we'll update this page and its "Effective" date. If the change is important, we'll also tell you in the app before it applies.
