Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026. This explains what data Flux uses, why, and your rights.

The short version:
  • Your videos, projects and recordings stay on your computer. We never upload them.
  • For your account we keep your email, a scrambled (hashed) password and a few account details.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is the independent developer of Flux, based in Romania. Contact: flux.editing.app@gmail.com. This policy covers the Flux desktop app, the website flux-editor.netlify.app and the Flux account service.

2. What stays on your computer

Everything you make in Flux is stored only on your own computer, including:

  • your projects, imported media, voice recordings and exports;
  • project version history (the Time machine) and your settings;
  • speech-to-text for captions, bleeping and silence removal. The model runs locally, so your audio isn't sent anywhere.

4. AI Editor

When you use the AI Editor, your typed request and a text summary of your project (such as clip names, lengths, positions, text and caption words) are sent through our server to Anthropic, which runs the AI model. Your video and audio files are not sent. We don't store the request or the answer. We only count how many times you used it that day. If you use your own Anthropic API key, the request goes directly from your computer to Anthropic. Anthropic handles that data under its own privacy policy.

6. Things the app downloads

To give you free content, Flux downloads files from other websites. Like any web request, these sites can see your IP address and that a download happened, but we don't send them your email or your projects:

  • Music from incompetech.com and the Internet Archive (archive.org), where the free songs are stored;
  • Fonts from Google Fonts;
  • The speech model for captions from Hugging Face (once);
  • Update checks and downloads from GitHub.

The website also loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

7. What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no tracking cookies on the website or in the app.
  • We never sell or rent your data, and we don't share it except with the services listed above or when the law requires it.
  • We take no payments, so we have no payment or card data.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account. Then it's removed from our account database straight away.
  • One-time codes: until used or after 15 minutes.
  • Server logs at our hosting provider: kept for a short time under Netlify's own rules.
  • Email copies: emails we send may stay in the sending Gmail account's "Sent" folder. We delete them on request.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • correct it;
  • have it deleted (you can do this yourself in Flux: Account → Delete account);
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • take it with you (data portability).

Email flux.editing.app@gmail.com for any of these and we'll answer within one month. You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro), or to the authority in your own country.

10. Children

Flux accounts are for people aged 16 and over. Younger users need a parent or guardian to agree for them. If you think a child under 16 created an account without permission, email us and we'll delete it.

11. Security

All connections to our server use HTTPS, and passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Sign-in codes are also stored hashed and are rate-limited. No system is 100% secure, so use a password you don't use anywhere else.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your data, we'll update this page and its "Effective" date. If the change is important, we'll also tell you in the app before it applies.
