Community Guidelines

Effective 24 November 2026. Flux is for everyone aged 13 and up - these rules keep it friendly.

The short version: be kind, be yourself, only share what's yours, and keep other people's private details private.

1. Where these rules apply

Everything other people can see: your username, profile picture, templates you share (their names, descriptions and titles), friend requests, and anything you send us in feature requests and bug reports. Your own videos and projects stay on your computer - but the Terms of Service still apply to how you use Flux.

2. Not allowed

  • Nudity or sexual content of any kind - and never anything that sexualises minors. We report child sexual abuse material to the authorities.
  • Hate and harassment: attacking people for who they are, bullying, threats, or targeting someone with friend requests they don't want.
  • Violence and dangerous acts: gore, glorifying violence, self-harm, or anything that encourages people to hurt themselves or others.
  • Pretending to be someone else: a person, a creator, a company or Flux staff. Only we can give badges like Verified, Support Team or Developer.
  • Private details: other people's real names, addresses, phone numbers, emails or photos without their permission.
  • Spam and scams: advertising, links to scams, fake giveaways, or creating lots of accounts.
  • Stolen work: sharing templates you didn't make, or copyrighted logos and characters in profile pictures you have no right to use.
  • Illegal content of any kind.

3. Badges

Badges (Founder, Developer, Verified, Bug Hunter, Contributor, Support Team, Sponsor, Content Creator, Educator) are given only by the Flux team. Premium is shown automatically. Don't copy badges into your username or picture, and don't ask other people to "give" you a badge - they can't.

4. Your privacy controls

  • Private account: only friends see your friends and followers, and only friends can follow you.
  • Friend requests off: nobody can send you new requests.
  • 2-step verification: keeps your account safe even if someone learns your password.

Find all of them in Flux → Settings → Account. Nobody - not even your friends - can see your email address.

5. Reporting a problem

See a profile or template that breaks these rules? Tell us in Flux → Settings → Report a bug (write the username), or email flux.editing.app@gmail.com. We read every report.

6. What happens if rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may remove a template, reset a profile picture or username, take away a badge, or ban the account (see the Terms of Service). If you think we got it wrong, email us and we'll look again.
