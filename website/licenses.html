If you'd like us to send you the exact source code for the versions in Flux, email flux.editing.app@gmail.com and we'll send it free of charge. Voices

The text to speech voices use the Piper "en_US libritts high" model, trained on the LibriTTS dataset by Heiga Zen, Viet Dang, Rob Clark, Yu Zhang, Ron J. Weiss, Ye Jia, Zhifeng Chen and Yonghui Wu, licensed under CC BY 4.0. LibriTTS is based on LibriVox public-domain audiobook recordings. The voice names in Flux (Ava, Max and so on) are our own labels, not real people.

Music
